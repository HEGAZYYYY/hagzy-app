Hagzy (حجزي) · User Privacy

Account & Personal Data Deletion

At Hagzy, you are always in full control of your personal data. In accordance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies, you can permanently delete your account and associated records at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Hagzy app on your device.
  2. Open the Profile (حسابي) tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Personal Data (حذف الحساب والبيانات الشخصية).
  4. Confirm deletion. Your profile, phone number, favorites, local cache, wallet details, and reservation records are cleared immediately across your device and Supabase Auth.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the Hagzy app, contact our privacy team and we will permanently delete your account within 48 hours.

Submit request via email:

Email: support@hagzy.app

Include the Egyptian phone number or email address linked to your Hagzy account.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Your personal profile (Name, Phone Number, Email).
  • Your Supabase Auth account session and login credentials.
  • Your reservation history, active holds, saved favorites, and local preferences.
  • Your stored wallet balance records and promotional claims.
