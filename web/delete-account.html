Hagzy (حجزي) User Privacy

Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

At Hagzy, we respect your right to control your personal information. In accordance with Google Play & Apple App Store Data Safety guidelines, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Hagzy app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile (حسابي) tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Personal Data (حذف الحساب والبيانات الشخصية).
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Forever (حذف نهائياً). Your profile, phone number, wallet balance, and reservation records will be erased instantly from our databases and Firebase Auth.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the Hagzy app, you can submit a deletion request directly to our privacy team. We will process your request and permanently delete your account within 48 hours.

Submit request via email:

Email: support@hagzy.app

Please include the 11-digit Egyptian phone number or email address linked to your Hagzy account.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Your personal profile (Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Avatar).
  • Your Firebase Auth UID and login credentials.
  • Your booking history, temporary holds, and past transactions.
  • Your stored wallet balance and promotional claims.
